AMRITA UNIVERSITY


        Mata Amritanandamayi (born 1953),known as Amma
        or "the hugging saint," is an Indian spiritual 
        leader and humanitarian recognized for her, selfless
        service and for embracing over 40 million people worldwide.
        Born in Kerala, she founded a global, volunteer-based movement 
        focused on, providing, education, healthcare, and aid for the
        poor.
