T20 FORMAT

Rohit Gurunath Sharma

Indian Cricketer

T20 CARRER
Rohit Gurunath Sharma is an Indian international cricketer and the former captain of the India national cricket
team in all formats of the game. He is a right-handed top-order batter. He represents Mumbai in domestic cricket
and Mumbai Indians in the Indian Premier League.

Key T20 International Profile & Stats:

  • Role: Right-handed opening batter, right-arm off-break bowler.
  • Matches: 159 (151 innings).
  • Total Runs: 4,231.
  • Average & Strike Rate: 32.05 average with a 140.85 strike rate.
  • Highest Score: 121*.
  • Centuries/Half-centuries: 5 / 32.
  • Sixes/Fours: 205 (most in T20I history) / 383.
  • Captaincy: Led India to victory in the 2024 T20 World Cup.
