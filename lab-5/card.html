SOLO LEVELING

Solo Leveling follows Sung Jinwoo, the weakest hunter who gains a unique power that lets him level up like in a game.
As he grows stronger, he uncovers dark secrets behind dungeons and monsters, becoming one of humanity’s most powerful fighters.

DEMON SLAYER

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba is an action anime about Tanjiro, a boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is
killed by demons and his sister Nezuko turns into one. He fights dangerous demons to save her and protect people. The anime
is known for its emotional story and amazing animation.

JUJUTSU KAISEN

Jujutsu Kaisen is a dark fantasy action anime about Yuji Itadori, a high school student who swallows a cursed object and
becomes the host of the powerful curse Sukuna. He joins a school of sorcerers to fight dangerous curses and protect people.
T he series is famous for its intense battles, strong characters like Satoru Gojo, and emotional storyline.
