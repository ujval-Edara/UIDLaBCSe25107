Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Amaravati, established in 2021 in Andhra Pradesh,

is a premier, NAAC A++ rated, research-intensive institution focused on engineering, computing, and management

Key Aspects of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham:
Rankings & Reputation: Ranked as one of the best research institutions in India and frequently in the Top 100 in the Times Higher Education Impact Rankings.
Campuses: Spread across India, with major locations in Amritapuri, Coimbatore, Bengaluru, Kochi, Chennai, Amaravati, and Faridabad (NCR).
Academic Offerings: Provides a wide array of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in Engineering, Management, Medicine, Dentistry, Nursing, Pharmacy, Ayurveda, and Humanities.
Research & Innovation: Highly research-focused with 180+ international collaborations and focus areas including AI, Cybersecurity, and Nanosciences.
Social Focus: Known for integrating education with values and offering outreach programs, such as AmritaCREATE, which works on technology for rural education
